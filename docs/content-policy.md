## Unpublished material

Files with `status: draft` generate no HTML, listing item, sitemap entry, copied asset, or feed. A noindex page is insufficient: draft bodies must not exist in deployment output. The earlier case-study outlines and taxonomy essay review stub remain drafts.

The original taxonomy essay remains withheld. Its legacy URL renders the current Writing index with canonical and noindex metadata. It does not republish the source or show an editorial review notice.

Public copy does not narrate its sourcing, editorial review, omissions, or unfinished projects. Keep that material in source documentation. Preserve necessary technical context in affirmative descriptions, including internal-build deployment and exact metric scope. Remove future-work sections rather than recasting them as completed results.

## Future publication gate

Before publishing new or expanded content:

1. Verify each claim against an identified source.
2. Confirm permission to disclose employer details independently of their presence in the résumé or old website.
3. Distinguish individual contributions, technical leadership, and broader team outcomes.
4. Preserve metric names, denominators, baselines, time windows, units, and limitations. Do not merge separate experiments or turn a percentage into percentage points.
5. Keep ongoing work distinct from completed results and internal deployments distinct from retail releases.
6. Review the final generated copy and confirm that source files, figures, and fixtures contain no uncleared material.

Do not invent publications, architectures, training experience, deployments, outcomes, team sizes, or ownership. Record unresolved factual and disclosure questions privately, outside the public repository.
